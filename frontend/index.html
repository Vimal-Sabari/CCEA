How it works

A cooperative coevolutionary search for the best defense per signature.

  1. 1
    Upload a report

    A CSV / XLSX where each row is a defense candidate — attack severity, defense strength, reduced severity — grouped by malware sample.

  2. 2
    Split into subpopulations

    Candidates are grouped one bucket per triggered signature. Each bucket evolves its own part of the defense.

  3. 3
    Assemble a combination

    Every generation picks one representative defense per signature (unexplored → random exploration → best-so-far), with occasional mutation, forming one complete defense strategy.

  4. 4
    Score it

    Scores are mapped to [0,1]. Fitness = (IS_Aggr − RS_Aggr) / IS_Aggr, where IS/RS are the RMS of attack severity and remaining severity across all signatures.

  5. 5
    Share the credit

    The combination’s fitness is recorded on every candidate that took part, so good team-players get chosen more often.

  6. 6
    Converge

    Repeat until every candidate has been tried and the best combination hasn’t improved for 20 generations.

  7. 7
    Result

    The highest-fitness defense combination for each sample, plus its convergence curve.
